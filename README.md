## Hi, I'm Lars.

I studied electrical engineering, got curious by HCI, and now spend most of
my time somewhere between hardware and software.

A couple of things here are worth opening:

- [studieschuld.help](https://studieschuld.help) started because I wanted a
  straight answer to an annoyingly complicated question about Dutch student
  loans. It runs entirely in the browser and does not track anyone.
- [transcriber-whisper](https://github.com/obywan-afk/transcriber-whisper) is a
  small desktop app for transcribing audio locally, without sending it to a
  cloud service.

The hardware side mostly lives on a bench or in private repositories.
[brailly](https://brailly.eu), a device for learning braille, is the closest
public window into that work.

Based in Helsinki and heading to Zürich. Away from the laptop, I am usually
bouldering, carrying an analog camera, or making modifications to
my espresso machine.
